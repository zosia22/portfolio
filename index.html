ALLEGRO CASE STUDY

Gift Discovery: Designing for the 'No-Idea' Shopper

Evaluating search guidance, layout inspiration, and checkout cross-selling loops in Poland's largest e-commerce marketplace.

UAM Laboratorium Umysłu Rozszerzonego

01 / Overview

Executive Summary & User Journey

1
Initial Search
Entering a general search query
2
Redirect Mismatch
Getting lost in subcategories
3
Hidden Inspiration
Invisible inspiration hub
4
Checkout Cart
Selecting items within budget (+30%)

Research Objective

Evaluate search relevance, navigation structures, and recommended accessories when a user is shopping for a gift with no prior idea.

02 / Methodology

User Profiles & Testing Setup

Research Parameters

  • Sample size: N = 5 Digital Natives (ages 21 - 27)
  • Duration: Average session of 40 minutes
  • Collaborators: Allegro UX Research Team & UAM coordinators

Task Scenarios

Task 1 (Inspiration): Find a gift for a family member starting with no specific product in mind.

Task 2 (Cross-selling): In checkout cart, add a accessory item within a 30% price limit.

Selected Insights

Detailed Usability Insights

Out of the 16 usability issues identified, the following section details 4 key findings that represent the most significant friction points for users and the highest potential business impact.

05 / Usability Finding 1

The Hidden Inspiration Page

High Severity
The Friction

Invisible Inspiration Hub

"Allegro has a very cool inspiration page. It's a shame that nobody actually knows it exists." Users searched external sites for ideas first.

The Redesign

Direct Homepage Banner

Integrate high-contrast entry-points for "Allegro Inspiruje" during gift seasons. Place pathways directly inside search auto-suggest fields.

06 / Prototype

Figma Interactive Redesign Solution

Interactive Showcase

Redesigned banners and search autocomplete pathways to bring the Inspiration Hub directly into the user's flow.

Key Outcome: Retains search traffic and converts lost "no-idea" shoppers into active marketplace customers.

07 / Usability Finding 2

Category Misdirection

Critical Severity
The Friction

Forced Non-Purchasing Blogs

"I clicked to buy handmade products, but all I got was DIY tutorials telling me how to make them myself." Users felt trapped and irritated.

The Redesign

Split Results UI

Establish a dual layout: present product purchases directly in the main feed, and house DIY articles in a distinct side tab.

08 / Usability Finding 3

Forced Autocomplete Redirection

Critical Severity
The Friction

Over-Specific Autoredirection

"I type 'Tourist' and Allegro redirects me to backpacks before I can even see search options." Restricts exploration for gifts.

The Redesign

Exploratory Autocomplete

Show multiple matching subcategory paths in the suggestions dropdown, allowing users to choose their own destination.

09 / Usability Finding 4

Storefront Lock Confusion

High Severity
The Friction

Invisible Seller Jail

"I searched for bookmark holders, and it said no match. Only later did I realize I was locked inside one specific seller's store."

The Redesign

Global Escape Button

Provide a clear alert stating: "No match found in *this store*. Search across all of Allegro?" with a prominent escape button.

10 / Takeaways

Participant Feedback Synthesis

User Praise

• Highly intuitive main shopping cart controls.

• Fast, responsive checkout pipeline speeds.

• Excellent review section for quality reassurance.

User Criticisms

• High cognitive load due to banner overload.

• Non-intuitive category naming structures.

• Ineffective accessory matches in shopping cart.
